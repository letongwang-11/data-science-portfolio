## Project Showcase

### Student Academic Performance Analysis

**Research question:** Which factors — study time, past failures, family support, and alcohol consumption — have the strongest association with final exam grades, and can a simple predictive model flag at-risk students early?

**Visualization:**

![Correlation heatmap between key factors and final grade](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/letongwang-11/data-science-project/main/visuals/correlation_heatmap.png)

*Figure 1: Correlation heatmap showing that past failures (r ≈ −0.36) and study time (r ≈ 0.14) are the strongest predictors of final grade.*

**Key findings:**
- Students with past course failures scored, on average, 3–4 points lower than their peers.
- A logistic regression model achieved ~75% accuracy in classifying at-risk students (final grade < 10).
- Family educational support showed a small but positive effect on performance.

**Limitation:** The dataset comes from only two Portuguese schools (2005–2006), so results may not generalize to other education systems or the current context.
